**Subject:** Nobody owns the digital half of a hybrid show

Hi Danica,

A pattern from producing 400+ virtual and hybrid events since 2020: on most hybrid shows,
the in-person side has a clear owner and the digital side has three partial owners. Marketing
owns the audience, ops owns the venue, and the AV vendor owns the stream — so the digital
attendee experience belongs to no one, and it shows.

That's a trade-show business problem rather than a production problem. It determines who
holds the budget, which line items get cut first when the show is over-committed, and why
the digital audience number is the one nobody defends in the post-show debrief.

I'd talk through what the ownership models we actually see look like, which of them hold up,
and the specific handoffs where hybrid shows break — speaker onboarding, run-of-show
transitions, and backup paths that were never rehearsed.

West Peek Productions started in Atlanta in 2020 and now also has a New York location.
Happy to be a source for a broader piece rather than a profile if that's more useful.
