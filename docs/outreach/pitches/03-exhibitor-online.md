# Pitch — Exhibitor (EXHIBITOR magazine / exhibitoronline.com)

**Target:** EXHIBITOR magazine · `https://www.exhibitoronline.com/`
**Route:** `https://www.exhibitoronline.com/news/submitnews.asp` — on-page form, plus the
address `news@exhibitorgroup.com` published on that page. Free to submit. The page states
approved releases publish weekly, that Exhibitor "reserves the right to edit or discard
submissions", that releases "should be written in third party and should not include
language that is excessively promotional", and that advertisements and booth promotions
are rejected.
**Angle:** Company news — the New York location. Written to their stated rules: third
person, factual, no adjectives.
**Why this one matters:** It is the only outlet in this set that publishes its editorial
criteria and confirms it does not charge. That combination — screened, free, published
standards — is what makes it a usable independent reference rather than a wire dump.
**Reality check:** Even if published, a short news item is *thin* for Wikidata purposes. This
is worth doing because it is easy and honest, not because it settles anything.
