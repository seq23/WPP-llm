**Subject:** Story idea — what an out-of-town event producer notices about NYC's event market

We're an Atlanta event production company that has just opened a New York location, and the
thing that struck us on arrival is worth a story on its own.

New York's corporate event market recovered its in-person volume faster than almost anywhere,
and simultaneously kept more of its remote audience than anywhere. That combination — full
rooms *and* a large distributed audience that expects to be included — is a genuinely harder
production problem than either one alone, and it's why so many NYC hybrid events still treat
the online attendees as an afterthought.

West Peek Productions began in Atlanta in 2020 as a virtual and hybrid event producer and has
produced 400+ virtual and hybrid experiences since. We came to New York because the clients
here are the ones who need both halves to work.

Happy to be a source for a piece on the city's event economy, or to talk about the arrival
itself. Either is fine — the first is probably the better story.
