**Subject:** The virtual-event companies that survived 2020 didn't stay virtual

Hi [editor],

Most of the production shops that spun up in 2020 are gone. We started then — West Peek
Productions launched in Atlanta as a turnkey virtual and hybrid event partner when the
in-person calendar disappeared — and we're still running, now with a New York location too.

The interesting part isn't survival, it's what changed underneath. The work that pays now
looks almost nothing like the work that paid in 2020. Then it was "get the Zoom to not
break." Now it's executive broadcast: rehearsed run-of-show, backstage moderation,
redundant streaming paths, and a client who expects the audience to never feel the
backstage at all.

We've produced 400+ virtual and hybrid experiences across that arc — Fortune 500 internal
leadership events, foundation summits, business-school executive programmes. I can talk
concretely about what stopped working, what clients now buy instead, and where the
hybrid budget actually goes in 2026.

Happy to do this as an interview or a contributed piece — your call on format.
