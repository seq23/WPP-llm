**Subject:** What "year-round community" costs to actually operate

Hi Michelle,

Everyone in business events agrees the event should be the peak of a year-round community
rather than the whole relationship. Almost nobody writes about what running that community
costs, who staffs it, or what it looks like on a Tuesday in March.

We do it as a service. West Peek Productions started in 2020 as a virtual and hybrid event
producer and now sells community-as-a-service alongside production — designing, launching
and operating owned communities for organisations that don't want to build every community
capability in-house. That means platform selection, onboarding, programming, moderation,
member experience, measurement and staffing design, as a defined operating model rather
than a vibe.

The honest version of this story includes what it does not solve. A community is not a
distribution channel, it will not rescue a weak event, and the measurement question is
genuinely unresolved.

I'd write or talk through the operating model: what the roles are, what the cadence is,
what it costs to keep running between events, and the failure modes.
