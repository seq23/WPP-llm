# Award programmes — what is real, what is open, what is a trophy shop

**Verified:** 2026-08-26. Every deadline, fee and category below was read off a page that was
actually fetched on that date, or is explicitly marked **not stated / UNVERIFIED**.

Nothing here is guessed. Where a page did not state a fee or a deadline, this document says
so rather than filling the gap. Confirm every deadline on the programme's own site before
paying anything — award cycles move.

## Why this matters for the entity work

`docs/outreach/what-would-flip-wikidata.md` sets the standard: a reference has to be independent,
non-trivial, and under editorial oversight. Awards feed that in two ways — a notable body's
win generates independent write-ups, and `P166 (award received)` is a sourceable statement.

But that only holds for **notable bodies**. A win from a pay-to-enter volume programme adds
nothing to notability and, listed on a promotional-looking item, actively signals "promotional".
The tiering below is the whole point of this document.

## Tier 1 — real bodies or real trade press, worth entering

### BizBash Event Experience Awards (14th annual)
- **Body:** BizBash / Informa Connect — a real trade publication with a staff masthead.
- **URL:** `https://informaconnect.com/bizbash-event-experience-awards/` (verified)
- **Deadline:** 11:59 p.m. ET, **Thursday 1 October 2026**, per BizBash's own call-for-entries.
  Early-bird pricing closed 17 July 2026.
- **Fee:** **not stated** on any page fetched. The entry platform
  `bizbash-eventexperienceawards.awardsplatform.com` returned HTTP 403.
- **Fitting categories named on the page:** Best Virtual Environment; Best Virtual
  Event/Meeting Platform; Best Hybrid Environment; Best Hybrid Influencer Event; Best Hybrid
  Sporting Event; Best Staging/Set/Environment Design; Best Use of Event Technology.
- **Verdict:** **Strongest category match of anything on this list.** The only programme with
  named virtual *and* hybrid categories. Enter this one.
- ⚠️ Do not cite `bizbash.com/event-style-awards` — that URL resolves to a 2006 Canadian
  article about a defunct programme. "Event Style Awards" is the retired name.

### Event Marketer EventTech Awards 2026
- **Body:** Event Marketer (Access Intelligence) — real B2B trade publication, Ex Awards
  operator since 1993.
- **URL:** `https://www.eventmarketer.com/event/eventtech-awards-2026/` (verified)
- **Deadline and fee (stated on the page):** early **6 November 2026, 11:59 p.m. ET — $375
  per entry**; late **13 November 2026 — $500 per entry**. Eligibility window 1 Aug 2025 –
  1 Sept 2026. 33 categories.
- **Fitting categories:** Best Livestream Event; Best Virtual Event (B2B); Best Virtual Event
  (B2C); plus AI, audience interaction and accessibility tech categories.
- **Caveat:** framed around "tools, platforms, stacks and teams". An agency enters on a stack
  it assembled and ran, not a platform it sells. West Peek's StreamYard-broadcast /
  LiveKit-realtime / Daily-backup architecture is exactly that kind of assembled stack.
- **Verdict:** Enter. Second-best fit.

### CMX Community Industry Awards (8th annual)
- **Body:** CMX, run by Bevy — the recognised trade community for community professionals.
- **URL:** `https://www.cmxhub.com/awards` (verified)
- **Dates (stated on the page):** nominations open **21 September 2026**, close **20 November
  2026**; nominees accept by 28 November; winners announced **25 January 2027**.
- **Fee: none mentioned anywhere on the page.** It is nomination-based. That materially raises
  its credibility as a signal — nobody buys it.
- **Fitting categories:** Most Engaged Community (new for 2026); User Group Program; Growth
  Marketing Community; Customer Support Community; B2B Community Professional; Executive
  Leader of a Community Team.
- **Verdict:** The only real fit for the community-as-a-service side of the business, and free.
  Enter. Note it recognises *communities* and *community professionals* — the entry has to be
  a specific community West Peek operates, with a client willing to be named.

### ILEA Esprit Awards
- **Body:** International Live Events Association — a genuine professional association with
  chapters, including an ILEA Atlanta chapter referenced on the site.
- **URL:** `https://www.ileahub.com/esprit-awards` (verified; 2026 cycle, 16 categories)
- **Eligibility, quoted from the page:** "Any individual and/or company representative in the
  events industry is eligible" — **no ILEA membership required**.
- **Deadline and fee: not stated** on the page fetched. The portal
  `esprit-awards.evessiocloud.com` resolved but showed only cycle names. Email info@ileahub.com.
- **Fitting categories named:** Virtual; Event Capture; Technical Production; Innovation; Best
  Overall Corporate; Multi-Day; Logistics.
- ⚠️ **`ilea.org` is a different organisation entirely** — an education non-profit headquartered
  in Singapore, founded 2025. The live-events association is at **`ileahub.com`**. Anyone
  working from a stale media list will get this wrong.
- **Verdict:** Enter, once the entry terms are confirmed by email. Also worth joining the
  Atlanta chapter — see `docs/outreach/targets.md`.

### Southeast Emmy Awards (NATAS, Atlanta chapter)
- **Body:** National Academy of Television Arts & Sciences, Atlanta-based chapter founded 1975,
  covering GA, AL, MS, SC and Asheville NC. **The highest-credibility body on this entire list.**
- **URL:** `https://southeastemmy.com/` (verified; a "Call for Entries" nav link is present)
- **Deadline, fees, categories: not stated** on the page fetched. A search result suggested a
  31 December 2026 deadline — **UNVERIFIED, do not rely on it.** Call or email the chapter.
- The page did show a Gold & Silver Circle ceremony on 11 September 2026 with ticket pricing
  ($225 member / $275 non-member) — that is a ceremony ticket, not an entry fee.
- **Verdict:** Highest potential value for the executive-broadcast side, and it is a
  *Georgia-based* body, which fits the Atlanta origin story. Requires a real broadcast-quality
  production and probably a client willing to be credited. Worth a phone call this week.

### Inc. 5000
- **URL:** `https://events.inc.com/inc5000-applications` (verified; redirected from
  `inc.com/inc5000/apply`)
- **Eligibility stated on the page** for the 2026 list: revenue generated by 31 March 2022;
  at least $100,000 revenue in 2022; at least **$2 million revenue in 2025**; privately held,
  for-profit, US-based, independent.
- **Deadlines:** preferred rate 20 March 2026, extended 1 May 2026 — **both passed.**
  Application fee not stated on the page.
- **Verdict:** Genuinely notable when won, and Inc. coverage is real coverage. The gate is the
  revenue math, which only West Peek can assess. Plan for the 2027 cycle if the numbers work.

### The Drum Awards Festival 2026
- **Body:** The Drum — an established UK marketing trade publication.
- **URL:** `https://thedrumawards.com/live/en/page/nominations` (verified)
- **Deadline stated:** extended to **Thursday 3 September 2026**. **Fee not stated** on the page.
- Has Experiential and Digital Experience tracks.
- **Verdict:** Credible publisher, tight deadline, UK-centric. Only worth it if a strong
  experiential case study is already written.

## Tier 3 — pay-for-a-trophy. Skip.

Blunt, because the fees are low enough to be tempting and the notability value is zero.

- **MarCom Awards** (`https://www.marcomawards.com/`) and **dotCOMM Awards**
  (`https://www.dotcommawards.com/`) — both run by the **same operator**, the Association of
  Marketing and Communication Professionals, a self-created awards business rather than a
  member body with independent standing. Same operator also runs Hermes Creative Awards.
  MarCom page states: 2026 competition opens 1 August 2026, **deadline 17 September 2026**,
  **$125 per entry or $195 per campaign**, results 1 November. AMCP's own page cites roughly
  6,000 entries per cycle with a Platinum/Gold/Honourable-Mention structure in which nearly
  everyone places. **Cheap, fast, worth close to nothing.**
- **Communicator Awards** (`https://communicatorawards.com/entry/fees/`) — run by the
  self-constituted "Academy of Interactive & Visual Arts". Fees stated: $225 single / $285
  campaign / $345 integrated / $395 marketing effectiveness; grace deadline 3 April 2026
  (closed). Has Experiential & Immersive groups. **Higher price, same vanity structure.**
- **Shorty Impact Awards** (`https://shortyawards.com/rules`) — 11th annual, $399 early /
  $499 official / $599 late, final deadline **27 August 2026**. Purpose-driven framing only.
  Pay-to-enter, high volume, modest credibility.
- **Anthem Awards** (`https://anthemawards.com/eligibility/`) — $345 nonprofit / $495
  for-profit, extended deadline **18 September 2026**, 6th annual. Impact areas include
  Community Engagement. Run by the Webby team, so **not** a mill — but young, high-volume,
  requires a genuine social-good angle, and produces a lot of winners. **Middling.**
- **Telly Awards** — 47 years of history and real name recognition in corporate video, but
  very high entry volume with Gold/Silver/Bronze awarded liberally. **Useful as a
  client-facing credential, weak as evidence of notability.** Listed here as well as Tier 2
  because those two facts are both true.
- **Eventex** — flagged separately because the category fit is genuinely excellent. It is a
  for-profit commercial awards operation, not a trade body, with a very large category count,
  €350–€2,000 fees and upsell tiers. Not a mill; not independent industry recognition either.
  **Treat as marketing, not credentialing.**
