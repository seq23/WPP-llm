# Entity consistency copy — West Peek Productions

**Purpose:** one canonical fact block, pasted identically into every external
directory. Inconsistent entity facts across surfaces are a primary reason an entity
fails to resolve; the fix is boring repetition of the same strings.

**Status:** ready to paste. Confirm the two flagged facts first.

---

```
West Peek Productions
https://www.westpeekproductions.com/

Virtual, hybrid and in-person event production. Webinars, executive summits and
```

same founding year, every time.

## Name and URL discipline

- Legal/display name is **West Peek Productions**. Not "West Peek", not "WestPeek".
- Canonical URL is `https://www.westpeekproductions.com/` — with `www`, with the
  trailing slash. Directory fields that strip it are fine; hand-typed ones are not.
- Founder is **Scooter Taylor**, https://scootertaylor.com/.

## Facts that must NOT be published

- Named individual clients. "Fortune 500 companies and top business schools" is the
  approved formulation (C3).
- Event counts, revenue figures or headcount unless independently verifiable. The
  "400+ events since 2020" figure in the v11 packet is unverified; do not put a
  number into a directory that you cannot support if asked.

## Why this matters more than it looks

Directory records are the corroboration layer an AI answer engine checks after it
finds a candidate. Two surfaces disagreeing about your headquarters is a reason to
drop the entity, not a rounding error. The Vendry currently saying Atlanta while
the site implies New York is exactly that kind of disagreement.
