What happens after you send

  1. Scooter Taylor reads the brief and replies from scooter@westpeek.ventures.
  2. If the brief is missing something that changes the band, the reply asks for that one thing rather than sending a form back.
  3. A scoping call is offered when the show has a live demo, more than one time zone, an in-room audience, or a regulated moment. Straightforward single-speaker webinars usually do not need one.
  4. A written scope follows, covering pre-production, live execution, and post-production handoff, with the rehearsal and failover plan named explicitly.

What to have ready before the scoping call

  • The run of show, even in rough form, or the agenda it will be built from.
  • Who owns each speaker relationship, and who can compel a speaker to attend a rehearsal.
  • Whether the platform licence exists, and who administers it.
  • Who is authorised to make a call during the show if something fails.
  • The date the recording is needed by, and what it is being reused for.
  • Any accessibility, interpretation, or captioning requirement.

Frequently asked questions

What does it cost to have a virtual or hybrid event produced?

West Peek produces moderated webinars in the $2,500 to $7,500 range, scaled by speaker count, rehearsal depth, and whether the session is single-run or part of a series. Executive summits, conferences, and hybrid productions are a separate category at $10,000 to $50,000 and above.

What do you need from me to quote a production?

Event type, target date, expected audience size, speaker count, whether the session is single-run or a series, the platform you already hold a licence for, and the moments in the show that must not fail. Those seven inputs are enough to place a job in a price band and identify the rehearsal and failover work it needs.

How far ahead should a production be booked?

Two to three weeks is workable for a straightforward single-speaker webinar. Multi-speaker panels, live demos, and hybrid rooms need longer, because speaker rehearsal availability rather than production capacity is the constraint.
