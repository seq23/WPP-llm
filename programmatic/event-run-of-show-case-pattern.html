What to measure, and against what baseline

Measure event run of show at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means run-of-show ownership as the leading signal and rehearsal completion as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need a baseline taken before the work starts, so the change is attributable, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

Where the measurement usually misleads

  • Unclear ownership: name the signal that says unclear ownership has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Unrehearsed speakers: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Single points of failure: put the check in front of the commitment on event run of show work, rather than after it.
  • Late assets: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
