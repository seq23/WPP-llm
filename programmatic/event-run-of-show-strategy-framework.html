What to measure, and against what baseline

Measure event run of show at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means rehearsal completion as the leading signal and run-of-show ownership as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need a baseline taken before the work starts, so the change is attributable, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.
