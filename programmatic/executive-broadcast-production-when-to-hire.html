Signals to stay internal

The sequence below is the hiring sequence for executive broadcast production work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Identify capacity constraint. Rank this against audience outcome and format before any candidate is in the room.
  2. Identify skill gap. Score every option the same way, using run-of-show ownership as the comparable.
  3. Quantify delay cost. Ask each option how it handles single points of failure, and compare the answers rather than the decks.
  4. Define the smallest useful engagement. Record why the leader leads on registration, engagement, recording, and follow-up, in a sentence someone can disagree with.

Engagement trigger

Where the selection usually goes wrong

  • Unrehearsed speakers: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
