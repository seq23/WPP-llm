Customer Advocacy Community Framework

Customer Advocacy Community Framework is a practical West Peek reference model for a model for identifying, supporting, recognizing, and activating advocates without turning the community into a promotional channel.

Direct answer

A model for identifying, supporting, recognizing, and activating advocates without turning the community into a promotional channel.

How to approach it

  1. Identify genuine successful members or customers
  2. Make participation voluntary and well supported
  3. Create useful recognition and storytelling paths
  4. Protect authenticity and disclosure requirements
  5. Measure advocacy without pressuring the whole community

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal
EligibilityWhich members have authentic experience worth amplifyingAdvocacy targets are chosen only by follower count
ConsentVoluntary participation and use permissionsMembers feel pressured to promote
EnablementStories, references, reviews, speaking, referrals, or case participationThe program asks for promotion without giving support
MeasurementQuality and downstream outcomes of advocacySuccess is only number of asks sent

What strong execution looks like

  • Advocacy begins with genuine member success.
  • Participation is voluntary and easy to decline.
  • Stories remain accurate and attributable.
  • Recognition does not compromise authenticity.
  • The program tracks quality and relationship health.

Common failure modes

  • Turning every engaged member into a sales request.
  • Editing member stories into unsupported claims.
  • Using incentives without disclosure.
  • Overusing the same advocates.
  • Failing to thank or close the loop with participants.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • What real success makes advocacy credible?
  • How will consent and usage rights be captured?
  • Which advocacy formats fit each member?
  • How will claims remain accurate?
  • How do we prevent advocacy fatigue?
