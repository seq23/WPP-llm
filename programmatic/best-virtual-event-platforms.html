Best virtual event platforms, compared by use case

The best virtual event platform is the one that fits the event: Zoom or Microsoft Teams for internal meetings and simple webinars, ON24 or Goldcast for marketing webinars that feed a CRM, Cvent or Bizzabo for conferences with registration and sponsors, and StreamYard for branded livestreams. Choose on audience size, interaction, integrations and who runs it live.

At a glance
  • Ten platforms compared below, from self-serve streaming studios to enterprise event suites.
  • Three cost models: per-host licences sized by attendee capacity (Zoom Webinars), self-serve monthly plans (StreamYard, Livestorm), and annual contracts quoted by sales (ON24, Cvent, Bizzabo, Goldcast).
  • The platform is not the production. Someone still has to own the run of show, rehearse speakers and keep a backup stream ready.
  • Moderated webinars: $2,500 to $7,500 per production (West Peek Productions published range, directional, not a fixed quote).
  • Executive summits, online conferences and hybrid productions: $10,000 to $50,000 and above (West Peek Productions published range).

How do you compare virtual event platforms?

Score each shortlisted platform against the event you are actually running, not against a feature list. These seven criteria decide most choices:

  1. Audience and access: internal sign-on, public registration, paid tickets, or invite-only.
  2. Scale: the attendee cap of the licence tier and how many people will be on stage at once.
  3. Interaction: Q&A, polls, chat, breakouts, networking and sponsor booths you genuinely need.
  4. Integrations: HubSpot, Salesforce or Marketo sync if leads matter; single sign-on if it is internal.
  5. Production model: browser studio only, or RTMP input from an external encoder such as vMix or OBS for broadcast-quality graphics.
  6. Accessibility and replay: live captions, recording, an on-demand library and transcript.
  7. Total cost: licence plus production plus the staff hours to run it, compared across the whole year.

Which platform fits webinars, summits, conferences and hybrid events?

  • Webinars: Zoom Webinars, Microsoft Teams webinars, ON24, Goldcast, Livestorm.
  • Multi-session summits: Goldcast, RingCentral Events, Airmeet, Zoom Events.
  • Conferences with sponsors and registration: Cvent, Bizzabo, Swapcard, Webex Events.
  • Hybrid events: Cvent, Bizzabo, Webex Events, Swapcard, with on-site AV feeding the stream (see the hybrid event platform guide).
  • Branded livestreams and town halls on social channels: StreamYard, Restream, Vimeo.

What else do people ask about best virtual event platforms?

What is the best virtual event platform?

There is no single best platform. Zoom and Microsoft Teams suit internal events and simple webinars, ON24 and Goldcast suit marketing webinar programmes, Cvent and Bizzabo suit conferences with sponsors and registration, and StreamYard suits branded livestreams. Match the platform to audience size, interaction and integrations.

What is a virtual event platform?

A virtual event platform is software that hosts an online event: registration, a live stage or webinar room, speaker backstage, attendee Q&A, polls and chat, sometimes networking and sponsor booths, then a recording and analytics. It does not plan or run the show; that is production.

What are the best platforms for virtual conferences?

For multi-track conferences with registration, agendas and sponsors, Cvent, Bizzabo, Swapcard and Webex Events are the usual shortlist. For lighter multi-session summits, Goldcast, RingCentral Events, Airmeet and Zoom Events cost less and are faster to set up.

What are the best hybrid event platforms?

Cvent, Bizzabo, Webex Events and Swapcard handle both an in-person and an online audience: check-in and badges on site, a streamed stage and shared Q&A online. The stream quality still depends on the on-site AV and production crew.

Who are the best virtual event production companies?

It depends on the event. Platform vendor services suit teams committed to one platform, AV companies suit camera-heavy hybrid shows, event agencies suit in-person-led events, and producers such as West Peek Productions suit high-stakes online events that need a run of show, rehearsal and a backup plan.
