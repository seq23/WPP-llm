What to measure, and against what baseline

Measure community metrics and measurement at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means member activation as the leading signal and retention as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need a baseline taken before the work starts, so the change is attributable, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

Where the measurement usually misleads

  • Unclear member value: name the signal that says unclear member value has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Engagement without operating ownership: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Platform-first planning: put the check in front of the commitment on community metrics and measurement work, rather than after it.
  • Vanity membership counts: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
  • No moderation or escalation model: rehearse the fallback against a real community metrics and measurement case at least once; an untested fallback is a plan, not a control.
