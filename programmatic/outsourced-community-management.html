Community management outsourcing: what to hand over and what to keep

Community management outsourcing means hiring an outside team to run day-to-day community work: moderation and member support, programming and events, onboarding, content prompts, reporting and escalation. You keep strategy, brand voice and final decisions; the partner works to written guidelines, response times and a monthly report. West Peek Productions offers this as Community as a Service.

At a glance
  • Keep in-house: strategy, brand voice approval and product decisions.
  • Outsource: moderation, member support, programming, onboarding and reporting.
  • Common platforms: Discord, Slack, Circle, Discourse, Khoros, Higher Logic and LinkedIn groups.
  • West Peek Productions quotes Community as a Service by scope (coverage hours, member volume, moderation risk, programming); it does not publish a fixed price.

How does outsourced community management work?

  1. Audit: members, platform, current activity and risks.
  2. Guidelines: tone, rules, escalation paths and response times, approved by you.
  3. Platform setup or clean-up: roles, channels, permissions and moderation tools.
  4. Handover or launch: a founding-member plan if the community is new.
  5. Run: daily moderation and support, weekly programming.
  6. Report: a monthly review of growth, engagement and issues.

What does outsourced community management cost?

Cost follows scope rather than a package price. The drivers are coverage hours and time zones, member volume, moderation risk, the amount of programming and events, and integrations. West Peek Productions quotes by scope; how to estimate it is set out in what Community as a Service costs.

What else do people ask about community management outsourcing?

What is community management outsourcing?

Hiring an outside team to run day-to-day community work, such as moderation, member support, programming, onboarding and reporting, while you keep strategy, brand voice and final decisions.

Can you outsource community moderation?

Yes. Moderation is the most commonly outsourced task: the partner works to your written rules, response times and escalation paths, and escalates sensitive cases to you.

Should you outsource community management or hire in-house?

Outsource to launch fast, cover more hours or fill a gap; hire in-house when the community is central to the product and needs deep product knowledge every day. Many teams start outsourced and hire later.
