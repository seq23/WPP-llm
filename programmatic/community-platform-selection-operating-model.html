Direct answer

Roles and decision rights

Community platform selection operating model is one decision inside community platform selection, and the job on this page is the narrow one: sequence the work so each handoff has an owner, an entry condition, and an approval. Two people can search the same topic and need different evidence, so the useful move is to say which part is standard, which part is contingent, and what the reader has to inspect first-hand.

Start with community purpose, audience, and member value. Set down where things stand now, where they need to be, and which constraints are genuinely fixed. Keep the commitment reversible while operating roles, governance, escalation, and internal ownership is still open, because an operating model has to hold on its worst week rather than on its first.

Operating cadence

The sequence below is the operating model sequence for community platform selection work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Name accountable owner. Name the owner and the entry condition for community purpose, audience, and member value.
  2. Limit approval ambiguity. Define what finished means here, in terms of repeat participation rather than effort spent.
  3. Document handoffs. Write the exception path for engagement without operating ownership before the stage runs, not during it.
  4. Define escalation thresholds. Hand this off in writing, so engagement, retention, measurement, and community-led growth survives the person who decided it.

Escalation and exceptions

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on community purpose, audience, and member value is a reason to narrow community platform selection work, not to produce more of it. Leaving operating roles, governance, escalation, and internal ownership unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once vanity membership counts is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.
