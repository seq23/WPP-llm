Hidden costs to surface

The sequence below is the cost sequence for virtual summit production work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Separate fixed and variable work. Price it including the internal time audience outcome and format consumes.
  2. Price internal labor and delay. Separate what is fixed from what varies with run-of-show ownership, and say which assumption drives each.
  3. Identify change-order triggers. Name the change that would move this number, and what single points of failure would cost if it landed late.
  4. Define the cost of failure. Tie a payment or approval to the observable completion of registration, engagement, recording, and follow-up.

Budget decision rule

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on registration, engagement, recording, and follow-up is a reason to narrow virtual summit production work, not to produce more of it. Leaving platform, broadcast, accessibility, and backup paths unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once unrehearsed speakers is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.
