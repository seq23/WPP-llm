What tells you the controls are working

Measure content marketing operations at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means conversion rate as the leading signal and learning velocity as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need the earliest signal that would have shown the problem, not the eventual symptom, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

The failure modes to design against
