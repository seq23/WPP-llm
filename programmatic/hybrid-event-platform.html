Hybrid event platforms: what they do and which to choose

A hybrid event platform is software that runs one event for an in-person and an online audience at once: registration, a live stream of the room, remote speakers, shared Q&A and polls, networking and replay. Cvent, Bizzabo, Swapcard, Webex Events and Zoom Events are common choices; production is what makes both audiences feel included.

At a glance
  • Platform and production are two purchases: the platform is software; production is the crew, cameras, encoder, run of show and backup stream.
  • Plan a hybrid conference six to eight weeks out; lock the venue AV and the platform before the agenda is final.
  • Executive summits, online conferences and hybrid productions: $10,000 to $50,000 and above (West Peek Productions published range).
  • West Peek Productions does not sell event software. It produces events on the platform you choose and can help you choose it.

What does a hybrid event platform do?

  • Registration and ticketing for in-person and online attendees in one list.
  • A live stream of the main stage, with captions and a recording.
  • Remote speakers joining the room through a backstage.
  • One Q&A and polling queue shared by the room and the online audience.
  • Networking, meeting booking and sponsor pages for both audiences.
  • On-site check-in and badges, an event app, and an on-demand library afterwards.

What does hybrid event production add to the platform?

The platform carries the stream; it does not create it. Hybrid event production supplies everything between the room and the online audience:

  • Cameras and room audio fed into an encoder, so remote viewers see and hear the stage properly.
  • A dedicated online host who speaks to the remote audience and brings their questions into the room.
  • Remote speaker rehearsal on the real setup before the day.
  • One run of show with cues for the room and the stream.
  • A backup stream and a second internet connection.
  • Captions, the recording and edited replays.

How much does hybrid event production cost?

West Peek Productions publishes a range of $10,000 to $50,000 and above for hybrid productions, summits and online conferences. The ranges are directional, not fixed quotes. What moves the number:

  • Number of cameras and rooms streamed.
  • Event days and rehearsal days.
  • Venue AV already in place versus brought in.
  • Remote speakers, interpreters and caption languages.
  • Edited replays and clips after the event.

Should you choose the platform or the producer first?

Bring the producer in first, or at the same time. The producer turns your goals into platform requirements (audience size, interaction, integrations, on-site check-in) and tests the shortlist against them, so you do not pay for a platform that cannot carry the show you are planning. What else do people ask about hybrid event platform?

What is a hybrid event platform?

A hybrid event platform is software that serves an in-person and an online audience at the same event: shared registration, a streamed stage, remote speakers, one Q&A queue, networking and replay. Common examples are Cvent, Bizzabo, Swapcard, Webex Events and Zoom Events.

What is hybrid event production?

Hybrid event production is the crew and planning that put an in-person event in front of an online audience: cameras, room audio, an encoder, an online host, remote speaker rehearsal, a run of show for both audiences and a backup stream. It runs on whichever platform you choose.

Which platform is best for hybrid events?

For large conferences, Cvent or Bizzabo; for networking-led events, Swapcard; for Cisco companies, Webex Events; for budget-conscious events with a Zoom-familiar audience, Zoom Events. Choose after you know audience size, interaction and on-site needs.

Do you need a hybrid events company or just a platform?

You need both. The platform is software you license; a hybrid events company provides the cameras, crew, online host, rehearsal and backup stream that make the online half feel like part of the event rather than a camera at the back of the room.

How far ahead should you plan a hybrid event?

Six to eight weeks for a hybrid conference: platform and venue AV in weeks one and two, agenda and speakers by week four, remote speaker rehearsals in the final week, and a full technical rehearsal on the day before.
