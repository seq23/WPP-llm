AI consultant vs AI agency: which one to hire, and when

An AI consultant advises: they assess your processes, pick use cases, choose tools and write the roadmap, billed by the day or project. An AI agency, or automation agency, builds and runs the workflows, integrations and agents, then maintains them. Hire a consultant to decide what to do; hire an agency to do it.

At a glance
  • Consultant output: an assessment, a prioritised use-case list, a tool choice and a roadmap.
  • Agency output: working automations, integrations and documentation, often with a support retainer.
  • Pricing models: day rate or fixed project for consultants; fixed build fee plus a monthly retainer for agencies.

When should you hire an AI consultant?

  • Leadership wants an AI plan but no one has mapped the processes.
  • You need a vendor-neutral view before buying tools.
  • Risk, data or compliance questions must be answered first.

When should you hire an AI or automation agency?

  • The use case is clear and the process is documented.
  • You need integrations built between tools such as HubSpot, Slack, Google Workspace and your CMS.
  • You want someone accountable for keeping the automations running.

Can one firm do both?

Many do, and it can work if the firm separates the assessment from the build and you can stop after the plan. Ask who wrote the roadmap and who will build it, and whether the plan recommends tools the firm resells. For marketing teams, see AI marketing workflows for where human approval should sit.

What should you ask either one before hiring?

  1. Which processes will change, and who owns them afterwards?
  2. What is the measurable outcome, and when will we see it?
  3. Where does a person approve AI output?
  4. What happens when a tool or model changes?
  5. What do we own at the end: documentation, prompts, accounts, code?

What else do people ask about ai consultant vs ai agency?

What is the difference between an AI consultant and an AI agency?

A consultant advises on what to do with AI: assessment, use cases, tools and roadmap. An agency implements it: builds and maintains the workflows, integrations and agents.

How is AI automation consulting priced?

Consultants usually charge a day rate or a fixed project fee for an assessment and roadmap. Automation agencies usually charge a build fee per workflow plus a monthly retainer for support and changes.

Should I hire an AI consultant or an automation agency?

Hire a consultant if you do not yet know which processes to automate or need a vendor-neutral view. Hire an automation agency if the process is documented and you need it built and kept running. The choice is the same in any country.
