Digital marketing agency workflow: the steps, with AI in the loop

A digital marketing agency workflow is the fixed path every piece of work follows: intake and brief, strategy, production, internal review, client approval, launch, then reporting and optimisation. An AI marketing workflow puts AI tools at named steps, such as research, first drafts, variants and tagging, with a person approving each output before it moves on.

At a glance
  • Seven steps, each with an owner, an output and an approval gate.
  • Common tools: Asana, Monday.com or ClickUp for the workflow; ChatGPT or Claude for research and drafts; Adobe Firefly or Canva for image variants; Zapier or Make for hand-offs; HubSpot or GA4 for reporting.
  • Human-in-the-loop rule: AI output is a draft until a named person approves it.

What are the steps in a marketing agency workflow?

  1. Intake: one request form with goal, audience, deadline and budget.
  2. Brief and strategy: the message, channels and success metric, approved before production.
  3. Production: copy, design, video and build, scheduled against capacity.
  4. Internal review: QA against the brief, brand and legal checklist.
  5. Client approval: one named approver and a feedback deadline.
  6. Launch: a launch checklist for tracking, links and channel versions.
  7. Reporting and optimisation: results against the metric, and the next change.

What is a marketing operating system?

A marketing operating system is the documented set of workflows, templates, tools, roles and meeting cadences that a marketing team runs on. Designing the operating model means deciding who owns each step, which steps are automated, and where approval sits, so work moves the same way every time instead of depending on who is asking.

How do you manage marketing, SEO and AI workflows together?

  • Run them through one intake and one calendar, not three.
  • Put SEO checks in the brief and the launch checklist, not after publishing.
  • Write an AI usage policy: which steps may use AI and who approves.
  • Review cycle time and quality monthly, and remove steps that add delay without catching errors.

When should you hire a marketing workflow consultant?

  • Work is commissioned in chat and nobody can say what is in progress.
  • Approvals take longer than production.
  • AI tools are in use but nobody has decided where or who checks the output.
  • You are scaling a team and need the workflow written down first.
