How to compare options

The sequence below is the selection sequence for brand messaging architecture work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Rank criteria before looking at vendors. Rank this against audience and category before any candidate is in the room.
  2. Separate must-haves from preferences. Score every option the same way, using sales-team adoption as the comparable.
  3. Test evidence against the exact use case. Ask each option how it handles inconsistent language, and compare the answers rather than the decks.
  4. Name the decision owner. Record why the leader leads on identity, channels, and rollout, in a sentence someone can disagree with.

Where the selection usually goes wrong

  • Inconsistent language: name the signal that says inconsistent language has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Design before strategy: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Generic positioning: put the check in front of the commitment on brand messaging architecture work, rather than after it.
  • Unsupported claims: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
  • No adoption plan: rehearse the fallback against a real brand messaging architecture case at least once; an untested fallback is a plan, not a control.

How this changes for SaaS

For SaaS, calibrate that to the team's real decision speed, internal depth, and stakeholder count rather than to a model borrowed from a much larger organisation. Decide which approvals are genuinely mandatory, which work can move asynchronously, and which evidence has to be retained once the people who made the decision have moved on.

The version of brand messaging architecture for SaaS companies worth writing down is the one that survives turnover. Record the criteria, not only the choice, so the next operator can see what would justify changing it.
