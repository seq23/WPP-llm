Audience growth strategy: channels, cadence and the metrics that matter

An audience growth strategy is a written plan for gaining and keeping the right followers, subscribers or members: who the audience is, the one or two channels you will own, a publishing cadence you can sustain, the formats that earn shares, how new people are captured into email or community, and the metrics you review every month.

At a glance
  • Six components (listed below), reviewed monthly.
  • A first plan runs 90 days: long enough to see a trend, short enough to change course.
  • Owned audience beats rented reach: count email subscribers and community members, not only followers.
  • Events and webinars convert attention into owned audience; West Peek Productions produces them from $2,500 per moderated webinar.

What goes into an audience growth strategy?

  1. Audience definition: who you want, what they need, where they already spend time.
  2. Channel choice: one or two primary channels you can do well, not every channel.
  3. Cadence: a publishing rhythm the team can keep for 90 days.
  4. Formats: the pieces that earn saves and shares in those channels.
  5. Capture: how new people move into email, a community or event registration.
  6. Measurement: a monthly review of the few numbers below.

How do events and webinars grow an audience?

A webinar or summit gives a reason to register, which turns followers into an email list, and the recording becomes clips and articles for months. The constraint is quality: a badly run event costs audience. See webinar production services for what a produced event includes.

When does an audience growth consultant help?

  • Followers grow but sign-ups and pipeline do not.
  • The team publishes on five channels and is good at none.
  • Nobody owns the monthly review.
  • You are launching and need an audience before the launch date.

What else do people ask about audience growth strategy?

What is an audience growth strategy?

A written plan for gaining and keeping the right audience: who they are, one or two primary channels, a sustainable cadence, the formats that earn shares, how people are captured into email or community, and the monthly metrics.

How do you grow an audience?

Pick one or two channels, publish on a cadence you can keep, double down on formats that earn saves and shares, and give people a reason to join an owned list, such as a newsletter or a webinar.

What is an audience strategy framework?

A framework of six parts: audience definition, channel choice, cadence, formats, capture and measurement, reviewed every month and reset every 90 days.

What does an audience growth consultant do?

An audience growth consultant researches the audience, picks channels and formats, sets the cadence and capture plan, and runs the monthly review until the team can run it alone.
