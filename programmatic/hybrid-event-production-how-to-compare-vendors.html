Reference checks

The sequence below is the vendor comparison sequence for hybrid event production work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Use the same questions. Rank this against audience outcome and format before any candidate is in the room.
  2. Score proof relevance. Score every option the same way, using run-of-show ownership as the comparable.
  3. Test senior ownership. Ask each option how it handles single points of failure, and compare the answers rather than the decks.
  4. Document why the winner won. Record why the leader leads on registration, engagement, recording, and follow-up, in a sentence someone can disagree with.

Final selection

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on audience outcome and format is a reason to narrow hybrid event production work, not to produce more of it. Leaving platform, broadcast, accessibility, and backup paths unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once late assets is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.

Where the selection usually goes wrong

  • Weak audience follow-through: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
