What tells you the controls are working

Measure marketing strategy consulting at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means conversion rate as the leading signal and qualified demand as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need the earliest signal that would have shown the problem, not the eventual symptom, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

The failure modes to design against

  • One-off campaigns: name the signal that says one-off campaigns has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • No feedback loop: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Channel-first planning: put the check in front of the commitment on marketing strategy consulting work, rather than after it.
  • Weak offer clarity: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
