Community Monetization Decision Framework

Community Monetization Decision Framework is a practical West Peek reference model for a decision framework for memberships, sponsorships, events, services, and paid access that protects member trust and value.

Direct answer

A decision framework for memberships, sponsorships, events, services, and paid access that protects member trust and value.

What this page recommends

  1. Protect the core member value proposition
  2. Choose a monetization model that fits member expectations
  3. Separate sponsor value from member manipulation
  4. Define access, benefits, and conflicts clearly
  5. Measure retention and trust alongside revenue

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal

Trust and disclosureClear commercial relationships and incentivesMembers cannot distinguish recommendation from promotion
EconomicsRevenue, cost to serve, retention, and operating burdenTop-line revenue is measured without member or operational cost

What strong execution looks like

  • Monetization follows proven member value.
  • Commercial relationships are disclosed.
  • Free and paid boundaries are understandable.
  • Sponsors or partners cannot override member trust.
  • Retention and satisfaction are monitored alongside revenue.

Common failure modes

  • Monetizing before the community has repeatable value.
  • Overloading members with sponsor inventory.
  • Creating paywalls that destroy the core network effect.
  • Using undisclosed affiliate incentives.
  • Ignoring increased moderation and support burden.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • What are members actually willing to pay for?
  • Which value should remain broadly accessible?
  • How will commercial incentives be disclosed?
  • What operating cost does the model add?
  • What trust signal would warn the monetization model is harming the community?
