Cost model

The sequence below is the ROI sequence for creative strategy consulting work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Define measurable benefit. Price it including the internal time business objective and audience consumes.
  2. Include internal labor. Separate what is fixed from what varies with approval cycle time, and say which assumption drives each.
  3. Include delay and risk. Name the change that would move this number, and what approval bottlenecks would cost if it landed late.
  4. State assumptions explicitly. Tie a payment or approval to the observable completion of distribution and reuse.

ROI decision rule

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on creative proposition and references is a reason to narrow creative strategy consulting work, not to produce more of it. Leaving asset plan, production constraints, and approvals unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once beautiful but unclear work is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.
