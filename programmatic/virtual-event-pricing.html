Pricing guidance

West Peek Productions' own published bands, not a market survey: moderated webinar productions at approximately $2,500–7,500, and larger executive summits, conferences, and hybrid productions at approximately $10,000–50,000+, depending on production complexity, speaker count, runtime, branding, platform requirements, and operator staffing.
