What to measure, and against what baseline

Measure AI knowledge systems at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means traceability as the leading signal and reliable rollback as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need a baseline taken before the work starts, so the change is attributable, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

Where the measurement usually misleads

  • No owner: name the signal that says no owner has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Silent failure: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
