Creative operations: the workflow, the team and the metrics

Creative operations, or creative ops, is the function that runs how creative work gets made: intake and briefs, resourcing and scheduling, review and approval rounds, asset management, tools and reporting. It lets designers, writers and video teams spend their time on the work, and gives marketing a predictable answer to when it will be ready.

At a glance
  • Five roles in a full creative ops team (table below); small teams combine them in one producer.
  • Common tools: Adobe Workfront, Asana, Monday.com or Wrike for work management; Frame.io for video review; Bynder or Brandfolder for asset management; Figma for design review.
  • Core metrics: cycle time, revision rounds, on-time rate and capacity use.
  • West Peek Productions scopes creative operations work per engagement; send the scope for a quote.

What does creative operations do?

  • Runs intake so every request arrives with a brief.
  • Schedules work against real capacity.
  • Sets review rounds and named approvers.
  • Manages finished assets so they can be found and reused.
  • Chooses and administers the creative tools.
  • Reports on throughput, cycle time and quality.

What does a creative operations workflow look like?

  1. Request through one intake form.
  2. Brief approved by the requester and the creative lead.
  3. Resourcing: assigned to a person with a due date.
  4. Production, with work in progress visible.
  5. Review: at most two rounds, with consolidated feedback.
  6. Approval by one named approver.
  7. Delivery and filing in the asset library, tagged for reuse.

How is a creative operations team structured?

RoleResponsibility
Head of creative operationsOwns the workflow, tools, budget and reporting
Creative producer or project managerRuns projects from brief to delivery
Traffic or resource managerSchedules work against team capacity
Digital asset managerFiles, tags and governs finished assets
Creative operations analystTracks metrics and finds where work stalls

Which creative operations metrics matter?

MetricWhat it shows
Cycle timeHow long a request takes from brief to delivery
Revision roundsWhether briefs and feedback are clear
On-time rateWhether the schedule is believable
Capacity useWhether the team is under- or over-loaded
Asset reuseWhether finished work is found and used again

When does creative operations consulting help?

  • Requests arrive by email and chat with no brief.
  • Review rounds routinely exceed two.
  • Nobody can say what the team is working on this week.
  • Assets are recreated because nobody can find the originals.

What else do people ask about creative operations?

What is creative operations?

Creative operations is the function that runs how creative work is made: intake, briefs, scheduling, review and approval, asset management, tools and reporting, so creative teams can focus on the work.

What is a creative operations workflow?

Request, brief, resourcing, production, review, approval, and delivery into the asset library, with one owner and a due date at each step and at most two review rounds.

What does creative ops mean?

Creative ops is short for creative operations: the people, process and tools that keep creative work moving on time and on brief.

How should a creative ops team be structured?

A head of creative operations, creative producers or project managers, a traffic or resource manager, a digital asset manager and an analyst. Small teams combine these roles in one producer.

What are creative agency operations?

The operating side of a creative agency: intake, scoping, scheduling, review, approvals, asset management and reporting across client work.
