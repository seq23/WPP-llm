Community Budget Planning Framework

Community Budget Planning Framework is a practical West Peek reference model for a budgeting model covering people, platform, programming, moderation, content, analytics, events, and contingency.

Direct answer

A budgeting model covering people, platform, programming, moderation, content, analytics, events, and contingency.

How to approach it

  1. List people and operating ownership costs
  2. Include platform, tools, moderation, and analytics
  3. Budget programming, content, and events
  4. Add launch, migration, and contingency costs
  5. Review cost per meaningful member outcome

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal
PeopleStrategy, operations, moderation, programming, analytics, and leadership timeOnly software cost is budgeted
TechnologyPlatform, integrations, analytics, automation, and migrationTools are bought without operating-cost implications
ProgramsEvents, content, facilitation, speakers, partnerships, and member supportProgramming is expected to be free labor
Risk and changeModeration spikes, migration, accessibility, legal review, and contingencyNo reserve exists for predictable operational change

What strong execution looks like

  • Budget maps to the operating model.
  • Internal labor is visible even when not invoiced.
  • One-time launch costs are separated from steady-state costs.
  • Contingency reflects moderation, migration, and program variability.
  • Spend is reviewed against member outcomes and operational health.

Common failure modes

  • Budgeting only platform subscription fees.
  • Ignoring executive and cross-functional time.
  • Underfunding moderation and member support.
  • Adding tools instead of fixing workflows.
  • Measuring efficiency only as cost per member.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Which people costs are hidden inside existing roles?
  • What is launch-only versus recurring?
  • Which programs require direct spend?
  • What contingency is realistic?
  • Which member outcomes justify continued investment?
