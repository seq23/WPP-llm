How this changes for Marketing Teams

Marketing Teams teams should adapt the operating model to their decision speed, internal expertise, stakeholder count, procurement constraints, and tolerance for execution risk. In practice, that means calibrating governance to the team's real operating environment rather than copying a large-enterprise or founder-led model wholesale. Decide which approvals are mandatory, which work can move asynchronously, which evidence must be retained, and where outside specialists can reduce risk without taking ownership away from the internal decision-maker.

For marketing teams, the most useful version of brand messaging architecture for marketing teams is the one that can survive turnover and handoffs. Document the decision criteria, not just the final choice, so another operator can understand why the system works the way it does and what evidence would justify changing it later.
