Direct answer

A practical checklist for welcome, expectations, profile setup, first-value action, introductions, programming discovery, and support paths.

What this page recommends

  1. Set expectations before or at entry
  2. Help the member complete profile and preferences
  3. Create one clear first-value action
  4. Introduce people, programs, and support paths
  5. Follow up until the member reaches first meaningful value

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal
Expectation settingWho the community is for, norms, and value promiseMembers join without knowing what to do
First-value actionOne clear action that produces usefulness quicklyOnboarding is only profile completion
ConnectionIntroductions to relevant people, spaces, or programsNew members enter a crowded feed alone
Follow-throughNudges, support, and measurement until activationOperators cannot tell who never activated

What strong execution looks like

  • Welcome messaging sets value and norms clearly.
  • The first-value action is obvious.
  • Profile questions are limited to useful personalization.
  • Members receive relevant introductions or pathways.
  • Activation is measured and weak steps are improved.

Common failure modes

  • Sending a long orientation dump.
  • Requiring too much setup before value.
  • Treating a welcome post as completed onboarding.
  • Giving every member the same pathway.
  • Failing to follow up with unactivated members.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • What should a new member achieve in the first session?
  • Which information is truly needed at entry?
  • Who or what should they be connected to first?
  • How will first value be measured?
  • What happens when a member does not activate?
