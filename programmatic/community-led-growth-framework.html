Community-Led Growth Framework

Community-Led Growth Framework is a practical West Peek reference model for a framework for turning genuine member value into advocacy, referrals, learning loops, and measurable growth without treating members as a lead list.

Direct answer

A framework for turning genuine member value into advocacy, referrals, learning loops, and measurable growth without treating members as a lead list.

What this page recommends

  1. Create real member value first
  2. Identify advocacy and referral moments
  3. Make sharing or inviting useful and voluntary
  4. Connect community insights to product and marketing
  5. Measure growth without exploiting member trust

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal
Value foundationRetention and member outcomes before acquisition scaleGrowth campaigns outpace community value
Advocacy triggerMoments when members naturally want to share or inviteReferrals are forced with incentives alone
Acquisition fitChannels and messages that attract the right membersGrowth optimizes volume over fit
Learning loopCommunity insight informs product, content, and go-to-marketMember feedback is extracted without closing the loop

What strong execution looks like

  • Retention and member value are healthy before aggressive growth.
  • Referral mechanics are useful and voluntary.
  • Acquisition messaging accurately reflects community value.
  • Member trust is protected when insights inform marketing.
  • Growth quality is measured beyond signup count.

Common failure modes

  • Treating members as a lead list.
  • Launching referral incentives into a weak experience.
  • Using private community conversations as marketing without permission.
  • Scaling channels that attract low-fit members.
  • Claiming community-led growth from correlation alone.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • What value makes a member want to invite someone?
  • Which acquisition sources produce retained members?
  • How is consent handled when using member stories or insights?
  • What growth would harm community quality?
  • Which member outcome connects credibly to business growth?
