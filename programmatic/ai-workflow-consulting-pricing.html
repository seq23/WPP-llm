What changes the price

The sequence below is the pricing sequence for AI workflow consulting work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Normalize proposals to the same scope. Price it including the internal time workflow and failure cost consumes.
  2. Identify assumptions and exclusions. Separate what is fixed from what varies with exception rate, and say which assumption drives each.
  3. Ask how revisions are priced. Name the change that would move this number, and what private-data leakage would cost if it landed late.
  4. Tie payment milestones to observable delivery. Tie a payment or approval to the observable completion of measurement, logs, and rollback.

How to compare proposals

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on measurement, logs, and rollback is a reason to narrow AI workflow consulting work, not to produce more of it. Leaving human checkpoints and exception handling unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once automating an unclear process is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.

What tells you the money is working

Measure AI workflow consulting at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means reliable rollback as the leading signal and time saved as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need assumptions and exclusions written down beside the number, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

Where the cost usually escapes

  • Automating an unclear process: name the signal that says automating an unclear process has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Automation without evaluation: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Silent failure: put the check in front of the commitment on AI workflow consulting work, rather than after it.
  • Private-data leakage: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
