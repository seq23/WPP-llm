Acceptance criteria

The sequence below is the deliverables sequence for marketing strategy consulting work, not a generic plan. Each step ends in something observable, so the next one starts from evidence rather than from momentum.

  1. Define format and owner. Write the boundary where audience and demand signal is decided, and name one thing it excludes.
  2. Define review standard. Give an example that clearly sits inside it and one that clearly does not, judged on conversion rate.
  3. Define dependencies. Say who owns the wording, because campaign and content system will be read by people who were not in the room.
  4. Define final handoff. Check it against one-off campaigns: a boundary that cannot rule anything out is not a boundary.

Handoff requirements

Tie the next move to what is actually known. Weak evidence on audience and demand signal is a reason to narrow marketing strategy consulting work, not to produce more of it. Leaving distribution, measurement, and iteration unresolved is what lets scope grow without an owner or a date. And once no feedback loop is visible, the honest move is a fallback or a smaller scope, before more money follows the plan.

What tells you the definition is holding

Measure marketing strategy consulting at two levels: the outcome the work exists to change, and the operating signals that move first. Here that means pipeline contribution as the leading signal and learning velocity as the one that shows whether the system underneath is healthy. Both need inclusion and exclusion stated as examples someone can check, and each should be attached to a decision - continue, narrow, change owner, or stop.

Where the boundary usually slips

  • No feedback loop: name the signal that says no feedback loop has begun, and the person expected to act on it.
  • Weak offer clarity: write the recovery step while it is still a choice: who reduces scope, who tells the stakeholder, and what gets rehearsed.
  • Channel-first planning: put the check in front of the commitment on marketing strategy consulting work, rather than after it.
  • Vanity metrics: assign it to a named person rather than to a meeting, so it is not left to whoever notices first.
  • One-off campaigns: rehearse the fallback against a real marketing strategy consulting case at least once; an untested fallback is a plan, not a control.
