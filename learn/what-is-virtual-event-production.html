Virtual event production: what it includes, who does it, what it costs

Virtual event production is the planning, technical setup, rehearsal and live running of an online event, plus the follow-up afterwards. It covers the run of show, platform and registration setup, speaker preparation, graphics, show calling, moderation, a backup stream and the recording, so attendees see a calm, intentional event rather than a meeting.

At a glance
  • Moderated webinars: $2,500 to $7,500 per production (West Peek Productions published range, directional, not a fixed quote).
  • Executive summits, online conferences and hybrid productions: $10,000 to $50,000 and above (West Peek Productions published range).
  • Timelines: three to four weeks for a webinar, six to eight weeks for a summit or online conference.
  • Staffing: one producer can run a simple webinar; summits and hybrid events need a show caller, speaker manager, moderator and platform operator.

What does virtual event production include?

  • Run of show: a minute-by-minute plan with an owner for every cue.
  • Platform and registration setup, with reminder emails.
  • Speaker preparation: briefs, technical checks and a full rehearsal.
  • Branded slides, lower thirds, holding screens and video packages.
  • Show calling and live switching on the day.
  • Q&A, chat and poll moderation.
  • A backup machine, connection and stream.
  • Recording, edited replay, clips and a results report.

How long does it take to produce a virtual event?

  • Webinar: three to four weeks from brief to live; faster for a repeat format.
  • Summit or online conference: six to eight weeks.
  • Hybrid conference: six to eight weeks, with venue AV locked early.
  • Replay and follow-up: within 24 to 48 hours afterwards.

Should you hire virtual event production services or do it in-house?

Do it in-house when the event is internal, low-stakes and has one or two experienced speakers. Hire production services when the event carries revenue, investor trust or executive visibility, has several speakers or sponsors, or your team would otherwise present and run the platform at the same time.
