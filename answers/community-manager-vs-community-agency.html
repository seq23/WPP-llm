Should you hire a community manager or a community agency?

Community as a service is an outsourced model in which an agency runs a business’s community as an ongoing service, covering strategy, programming, events, content, moderation and reporting, instead of the business hiring that whole team itself. The alternative is an in-house community manager: one employee who owns the community day to day.

Quick answer

Hire an in-house community manager when the community is core to your business, needs someone every day, and you can fund a full-time role: published US data puts average base pay at about $66,000 to $69,000 a year before benefits. Use a community agency, or community as a service, when you need several skills at once, want to test the idea before hiring, or cannot keep a full-time person busy.

What this guide is best for: business owners and marketing leads deciding who should build and run a customer or brand community.

What does a community manager do?

A community manager brings members together and keeps them coming back.

  • Programming: plans the meetups, live sessions, threads and challenges that give members a reason to return
  • Moderation: sets and enforces the rules, and keeps the space safe and useful
  • Connection: welcomes new members and introduces people to each other
  • Feedback: carries what members say back to product, support and marketing
  • Reporting: tracks whether members return, contribute and refer others

What is community as a service?

Community as a service means hiring an outside team to run your community as an ongoing service rather than a one-off project.

West Peek Productions describes itself as a Community-as-a-Service and creative agency that helps organizations build, engage and grow communities “through strategy, experiences, storytelling, brand, content, and audience growth” (westpeekproductions.com). The phrase is rare in search: Google often reads it as “community service”, which is volunteer work and a different thing.

How do you decide?

  1. Write down the work. List the events, threads, content and moderation the community needs each month.
  2. Count the hours. If the list does not fill a full-time week, a hire will sit idle part of the time.
  3. Count the skills. If it needs event production, writing, design and strategy, one person rarely has all of them.
  4. Price both. Full yearly cost of a hire against a written agency scope for the same list.
  5. Decide who owns the relationships. Whatever you choose, someone inside the business should know the members.

Is a community manager the same as a social media manager?

No. A social media manager publishes to an audience on social platforms; a community manager gets members talking to each other, often in a space the business hosts. The roles overlap when a community lives inside a social media group, and small teams often give both jobs to one person.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a community manager cost?

Published US salary data puts an online community manager’s average base pay at about $65,748 a year (PayScale, updated April 2025), with most between $44,000 and $100,000. Built In lists $68,770. Benefits add to that: in private industry, wages are 70 percent of what employers spend per worker (BLS, June 2026).

What is community as a service?

Community as a service is an outsourced model in which an agency runs a business’s community as an ongoing service, covering strategy, programming, events, content, moderation and reporting, instead of the business hiring that whole team itself. The business keeps the decisions and the relationships; the agency does the running.

Is a community manager the same as a property or HOA community manager?

No. A brand or online community manager runs a group of customers or members. A property or community association manager runs buildings and homeowners associations; the US Bureau of Labor Statistics counts that as a separate job. Many “community manager” salary searches show the property job, so check which one a figure describes.

What does a community agency cost?

There is no reliable public price list. Community agencies usually quote by scope: how many programmes, events, channels and members they run each month. Ask for a written scope and compare it with the full yearly cost of a hire, salary plus benefits, tools and management time.
