What affects virtual event production cost?

Direct answer

Virtual event production cost depends on event length, number of speakers, rehearsal needs, sponsor requirements, platform complexity, creative assets, and whether the team needs post-event repurposing.

The two bands, and what moves a job inside one

West Peek Productions publishes two price bands. They are the agency’s own rates, not a market survey, and no third-party benchmark is quoted here because none has been verified for this page.

 Moderated bandSummit and hybrid band
Published range$2,500 to $7,500$10,000 to $50,000 and above
What starts hereA webinar, an executive broadcast, or a single-room virtual event.An online conference, a summit, or a hybrid event with an in-room audience.
Low end of the bandOne speaker, one rehearsal, a straightforward deck.A single track, one venue room, no concurrent sessions.
High end of the bandMulti-speaker panels, live demos, interpretation, or a redundant backup stream.Multiple concurrent tracks, in-room technical scope, and full redundancy posture.
What moves the figureSpeaker count, rehearsal depth, and whether the session is single-run or part of a series.Session count, in-room technical scope, redundancy, and how many tracks run at once.
What the band does not decideWhich platform is used, and how many producers the show needs on the day.Whether the venue’s own AV contract is included; that is scoped separately.

Both ranges are West Peek Productions’ published bands; the production scoping calculator is where they are published and where the answers that move a job inside a band are set out. Directional only, not a fixed quote. A supplier who names a flat rate without asking about speaker count, rehearsal expectations, or failover requirements has not scoped the event.

Best for

  • founders and operators with lean teams
  • teams that need polished execution without bloated process
  • commercial pages, launches, summits, webinars, or credibility assets

Not for

  • teams looking only for a software subscription
  • work that needs no human review
  • projects with no owner, deadline, or decision path

Red flags

  • no single approver
  • unclear audience or offer
  • no rehearsal or QA time before launch
