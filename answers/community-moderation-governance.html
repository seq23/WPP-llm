Community authority · governance

What should a community moderation and governance model include?

Community governance should define acceptable behavior, moderator authority, escalation paths, response times, privacy boundaries, conflict handling, member appeals where appropriate, and who can change policy. Moderation is an operating system, not just deleting posts.

Direct answer

Community governance should define acceptable behavior, moderator authority, escalation paths, response times, privacy boundaries, conflict handling, member appeals where appropriate, and who can change policy. Moderation is an operating system, not just deleting posts.

How to approach it

  1. Define behavior rules and member expectations
  2. Set moderator authority and severity levels
  3. Create escalation and documentation rules
  4. Clarify privacy, appeals, and policy ownership
  5. Review incidents and update the playbook

Decision framework

DecisionWhat to evaluateFailure signal
Behavior standardsWhat is allowed, discouraged, and prohibitedRules are vague or applied inconsistently
Moderator authorityWhat moderators can remove, warn, restrict, or escalateModerators lack clear decision rights
Severity and escalationHow incidents are classified and routedSerious cases are handled ad hoc
Documentation and appealsWhat is recorded, retained, communicated, and reviewableDecisions cannot be explained or audited

What strong execution looks like

  • Rules use examples and are accessible before conflict occurs.
  • Moderators share a severity model and escalation path.
  • Privacy and safety boundaries are explicit.
  • Incidents are documented consistently.
  • Policy changes are reviewed after recurring patterns or serious incidents.

Common failure modes

  • Writing rules only after the first crisis.
  • Letting high-status members receive different enforcement.
  • Escalating every minor issue to executives.
  • Keeping no incident record.
  • Confusing healthy disagreement with harassment or abuse.

Questions to ask before deciding

  • Which behaviors create the highest member risk?
  • What can moderators decide independently?
  • What requires legal, safety, or executive escalation?
  • How are members informed about enforcement?
  • How will policy effectiveness be reviewed?
