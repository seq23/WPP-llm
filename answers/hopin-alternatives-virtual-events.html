What are the best Hopin alternatives for virtual events in 2026?

A Hopin alternative is a virtual event platform that does the jobs Hopin Events did in one place: a main stage with a live stream, smaller breakout rooms, one-to-one networking, sponsor booths, live chat and replays. In 2026 the right choice depends on whether you need the whole venue or only part of it.

Quick answer

Hopin’s events platform now belongs to RingCentral, which bought Hopin’s Events and Session products in August 2023 and sells the platform as RingCentral Events. If you want the closest match, start there. If you need the full venue with a crew to run it, West Peek Productions runs shows on westpeek.live, which is in early access. If you only need to broadcast, a streaming studio such as StreamYard is enough.

What this guide is best for: event teams who used Hopin for a conference or summit and need to pick a new home for their next one.

What happened to Hopin?

Hopin sold its events business to RingCentral in 2023 and its remaining products to Bending Spoons in 2024.

  • 2 August 2023: RingCentral announced it was acquiring Hopin’s flagship Events platform and its Session product, along with their customers and the staff who built them (SiliconANGLE).
  • November 2023: RingCentral presented the platform as RingCentral Events, “part of our recent asset acquisition from Hopin” (RingCentral blog).
  • After the sale: Hopin kept StreamYard, a video hosting product and Superwave (SiliconANGLE).
  • 9 April 2024: Bending Spoons announced it would acquire StreamYard (Wikipedia, citing Business Wire).

What do former Hopin users need from a new platform?

Most teams need the same six rooms Hopin gave them, plus a crew they can rely on during the show.

  • Main stage with a live stream that everyone watches together
  • Breakout rooms for smaller sessions running alongside the stage
  • One-to-one networking: timed video calls with someone new
  • Expo booths where sponsors show what they do
  • Live chat for the whole event and for each room
  • Replays of each session after the show
  • Someone running the show: speaker checks, cues and backup if a stream drops

What does westpeek.live look like?

westpeek.live is the platform West Peek Productions runs shows on. It is in early access. The public demo venue has rooms for the stage, breakouts, 4-minute speed networking, chat, expo and replay, and you can walk through it without signing up: westpeek.live/venue/demo/lobby.

The demo is a sample event with made-up speakers and sponsors. Attendee sign-ups are off. Shows on westpeek.live are booked through West Peek Productions.

How do I choose a Hopin replacement?

  1. List the rooms you used. If you only used the stage, a streaming studio may be enough.
  2. Decide who runs the show. Self-serve software needs your own producer on the day.
  3. Check networking. If 1:1 meetings were the reason people came, test the matching in a demo.
  4. Ask about replays. Find out when recordings appear and who can watch them.
  5. Run a rehearsal on the platform with your real speakers before you announce the date.

Frequently asked questions

Is Hopin still available?

Hopin’s events platform was sold to RingCentral in 2023 and is sold today as RingCentral Events. Hopin’s other products, including StreamYard, went to Bending Spoons in 2024. If you had a Hopin Events account, RingCentral is the company that now runs that platform.

Is West Peek Productions connected to Hopin or RingCentral?

No. West Peek Productions is an independent event production agency and has no relationship with Hopin, RingCentral, Zoom, Airmeet, StreamYard or Bending Spoons. We name those products only to compare them, so you can choose the right one for your event.

Can I try westpeek.live?

Yes, you can walk through the public demo venue at westpeek.live/venue/demo/lobby without signing up. It is a sample event with made-up speakers and sponsors. To run a real show on the platform, send West Peek Productions your date and audience through the production plan form.

What does a produced virtual event cost?

West Peek Productions publishes its ranges: about $2,500 to $7,500 for a moderated webinar and $10,000 to $50,000 or more for a summit or online conference, depending on length, speakers and sponsors.
