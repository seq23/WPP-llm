Who publishes this site

West Peek Productions is a virtual, hybrid and executive broadcast production company, founded in 2020. It plans, rehearses and runs high-stakes online events: investor and board meetings, webinars, online conferences, summits and leadership broadcasts. On those jobs it owns the run of show, speaker rehearsal, platform setup and the backup room, so nothing important is improvised on the day.

The company works from New York, where it is headquartered, and from Atlanta and Memphis. Commercial work is quoted and contracted at westpeekproductions.com; this site is the library, not the storefront.

The company name is spelled West Peek Productions. It is also searched as West Peak Productions or WestPeak.

Who writes it

Editorial direction is owned by Scooter Taylor, founder of West Peek Productions (LinkedIn). Pages are published under the company as publisher and credit him as author.

What that means in practice: the people who run the events decide what the pages say. The subjects this site writes about — virtual event production, hybrid and broadcast production, community strategy and the agency-selection questions around them — are the subjects the company is hired to do.

How these pages are written and kept honest

  1. Questions come from measured demand. Pages are built against questions Search Console shows people actually asking, not a keyword wishlist.
  2. Trade-offs are named. A comparison page says where each option is the wrong answer. A pricing page says what moves the number.
  3. No invented credentials. The site asserts only facts it can stand behind: the company, its founder, where it works and how to reach it. There are no fabricated awards, client logos or certifications anywhere on this site.
  4. Pages carry a date and get corrected. When a page is wrong, it is fixed at the source that generates it, not patched in place.

How to reach a person

For a production quote, a rehearsal plan or a second opinion on an event that has to go right: scooter@westpeek.ventures. For commercial enquiries and scoping, the inquiry form lives on westpeekproductions.com.

If a page here is wrong, say so in that email and name the URL. Corrections are made at the generator, so the fix holds the next time the site rebuilds.
