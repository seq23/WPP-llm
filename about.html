Who publishes this site

West Peek Productions is a virtual, hybrid and executive broadcast production company, founded in 2020, and today also a Community-as-a-Service and creative agency that helps organizations build, engage and grow communities. On events, it plans, rehearses and runs high-stakes online shows: investor and board meetings, webinars, online conferences, summits and leadership broadcasts. On those jobs it owns the run of show, speaker rehearsal, platform setup and the backup room, so nothing important is improvised on the day.

The company works from New York, where it is headquartered, and from Atlanta and Memphis. Commercial work is quoted and contracted at westpeekproductions.com; this site is the library, not the storefront.

The company name is spelled West Peek Productions. It is also searched as West Peak Productions or WestPeak.

Who writes it

Editorial direction is owned by Scooter Taylor. Pages are published and credited under West Peek Productions.

What that means in practice: the people who run the events and the community work decide what the pages say. The subjects this site writes about — virtual event production, hybrid and broadcast production, community strategy and the agency-selection questions around them — are the subjects the company is hired to do.

How these pages are written and kept honest

  1. Questions come from measured demand. Pages are built against questions Search Console shows people actually asking, not a keyword wishlist.
  2. Trade-offs are named. A comparison page says where each option is the wrong answer. A pricing page says what moves the number.
  3. No invented credentials. The site asserts only facts it can stand behind: the company, its founder, where it works and how to reach it. There are no fabricated awards, client logos or certifications anywhere on this site.
  4. Pages carry a date and get corrected. When a page is wrong, it is fixed at the source that generates it, not patched in place.
