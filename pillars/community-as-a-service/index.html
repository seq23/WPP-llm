Community as a Service authority library

Community as a Service is an outsourced operating model that helps a company design, launch, operate, measure, and improve an owned community without building every capability internally. This library covers the buyer, strategy, operating, platform, staffing, governance, engagement, retention, and measurement questions companies ask when building a brand or company community.
